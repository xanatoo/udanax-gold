PURPOSE This procedure provides background information
        on the hazards of vacant buildings.

UNDERWRITING    Vacant buildings present both a
                physical and a moral hazard.

The physical hazards exist due to the lack of
maintenance and upkeep that usually exist at such
locations. It should be noted that the longer the
building is left in a vacant condition the greater
the deterioration to be expected.

The construction of the building is a significant
component in the underwriting of this coverage
(refer to Property Underwriting Guide for
construction details). The construction needs to
be evaluated when evaluating the potential
exposures to adjacent structures from the property
damage as well as the life safety view.

The moral hazard exists due to the vacant building
being a cash drain and therefore possibly being
subject to arson. With a vacant building the moral
hazard has to be investigated thoroughly.
